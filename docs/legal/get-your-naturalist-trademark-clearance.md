## 1. Scope and Assumptions

The intended goods/services for GET YOUR NATURALIST were not specified. This clearance assumes the mark would be used for one or more of the following (the analysis flags where the risk profile changes by field):

- **Class 9 / 42** — downloadable or SaaS software, e.g., an app or AI assistant that identifies plants/wildlife or connects users with naturalist expertise;
- **Class 41** — nature education, guided outdoor programs, naturalist training or certification content;
- **Class 39 / 35** — a marketplace or booking service for hiring naturalist guides or nature tours.

If the actual goods/services differ materially (e.g., supplements, apparel, food), the conflict analysis in Section 4 must be redone — several of the closest live marks sit in exactly those classes.

### Search methodology and limitations

Searches were run against web-indexed mirrors of the USPTO register (Justia Trademarks, Trademark Elite, uspto.report, Trademarkia) and general web/common-law searching. Direct queries to USPTO TESS and the paid databases were not available from this environment. **Not covered:** state trademark registers, design/logo marks, phonetic-equivalent and foreign-language variants beyond those noted, business-name registries, and non-indexed common-law uses. A full professional search must precede any final opinion.

## 4. Likelihood-of-Confusion Analysis (abbreviated *DuPont* factors)

**"NATURALIST" is a weak, crowded term.** The register and marketplace show many coexisting NATURALIST-formative marks across classes. Where a shared term is descriptive of the services and diluted by third-party use, each mark's protection is narrow and confusion typically requires close similarity in both marks *and* goods/services. This materially favors clearance.

Field-by-field risk:

1. **Nature-ID / naturalist-assistant app or online community — MODERATE risk.** INATURALIST is live, strong through use, and semantically close ("i" + naturalist vs. "get your" + naturalist, both signaling access to naturalist capability via an app). A consumer-facing nature app under GET YOUR NATURALIST invites an opposition or at least a demand letter from a well-resourced, protective nonprofit. Coexistence is arguable (different structure, descriptive shared term) but this is the conflict to take seriously.
2. **Tour/guide booking marketplace — MODERATE risk.** GETYOURGUIDE shares the identical syntactic formula and adjacent semantics (a naturalist *is* a kind of guide). For a booking marketplace for outdoor guides, expect GetYourGuide GmbH — an active enforcer — to object on a family-of-marks / same-commercial-impression theory.
3. **Nature education / naturalist training (IC 41) — LOW-TO-MODERATE risk.** The field is crowded with descriptive "naturalist" program names (Master Naturalist etc.), which cuts both ways: little confusion risk, but also weak exclusivity.
4. **Bird feed, supplements, toys, apparel — CONFLICT.** The live NATURALIST (bird feed), NATURALISTA (toys/sporting goods), and pending NATURALIST applications (supplements; probable apparel) would each be a direct obstacle in their class. **Do not use the mark on these goods without further analysis**, including merchandise (t-shirts, hats) sold under the brand — the Berdels application must be reviewed first.

---

## 5. Registrability Assessment

Beyond conflicts, there is a **meaningful descriptiveness risk under Section 2(e)(1)**. If the services are literally "providing/connecting users with naturalists," GET YOUR NATURALIST arguably just describes the service ("get yourself a naturalist"), much like GET YOUR GUIDE for guide booking. Expect a possible merely-descriptive refusal or a required disclaimer of "NATURALIST." Mitigations: argue the mark is at most suggestive as a unitary slogan; accept a disclaimer; or fall back to the Supplemental Register with a later Section 2(f) acquired-distinctiveness claim.

## 6. Draft Opinion (for attorney review and signature)

Subject to the limitations in Section 1 and verification of the pending applications identified in Section 3, it is our preliminary view that:

1. **Use** of GET YOUR NATURALIST for nature-education services and related software presents an **acceptable but not risk-free** clearance profile. No identical or near-identical mark was found; the shared element NATURALIST is descriptive and diluted. The residual risk is concentrated in (a) INATURALIST for app/community offerings and (b) GETYOURGUIDE for any booking-marketplace positioning, either of which could pursue an objection that would be defensible but costly.
2. **Registration** on the Principal Register is **uncertain**: a Section 2(e)(1) descriptiveness refusal is reasonably likely depending on the services identification, and the pending NATURALIST applications (SN 98/286,984; SN 99/356,535) could be cited under Section 2(d) if the goods overlap.
3. **Use on bird feed, supplements, toys/sporting goods, or apparel is not cleared** on the current record.
